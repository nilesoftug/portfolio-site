Kadhi

A streaming platform for music, video, speeches and drama — made for East African creators and listeners.

Artists and creators publish their work; listeners play it on the web or on their phones. Kadhi is built for real-world connections: it retries on weak networks, lets you download for offline listening, and keeps playing with your screen off.

Visit kadhi.nilesoftug.com

  • Music, speeches & drama

    Audio and video content in one place, with playlists and a queue you control.

  • Switch audio ⇄ video

    Watch when you can, listen when you can't — the song carries on from the same second.

  • Offline listening

    Download tracks to your phone and play them with no data. Your own files play too.

  • Reactions & comments

    Listeners respond to what they hear, and artists see it.

  • Web & Android

    Use it in a browser or install the Android app. Notification and lock-screen controls included.

  • Made for our region

    Prices in Ugandan shillings and a multilingual interface.

Who we are

A Ugandan company building for East Africa

Nilesoft UG is a technology company based in Uganda. We build software, teach digital and AI skills, and do the data work that machine learning depends on.

Much of the software people use wasn't designed with our reality in mind: patchy connections, limited data, phones with little storage, many languages. We start from those conditions, and our products and training reflect them.

  • 01

    Built for real conditions

    Light apps, offline options and graceful behaviour on weak networks.

  • 02

    Local first

    Local content, local languages and prices in shillings, designed with the region in mind.

  • 03

    Skills that last

    Training should leave people able to do something new on their own.

  • 04

    Shipped, not promised

    We show working software, like Kadhi, that people can use today.
